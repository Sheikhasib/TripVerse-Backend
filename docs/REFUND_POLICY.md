# TripVerse Refund Policy

**Applies to:** all paid tour package bookings made through TripVerse.
**Last updated:** August 2026

---

## 1. The basic rule

Once a booking is **paid and confirmed**, it cannot be cancelled directly from your account.
Your money is committed to securing your seat, hotels, and operator arrangements.

If you cannot travel, you may **apply for a refund**. Every application is reviewed by our
team, and refunds are issued strictly according to the policy below — provided the facts
check out.

> Bookings that are still **unpaid (Pending)** can be cancelled freely at any time, since no
> money has been collected.

## 2. How to apply for a refund

1. Go to **Dashboard → My Bookings** and open the paid booking.
2. Click **Request Refund**.
3. Select the **reason category**, describe the situation, and attach supporting documents
   (photo/scan) where required.
4. Submit. You will receive email and in-app updates at every step.

**Rules of application**

- One active application per booking.
- If your application is rejected, you may re-apply **once** with new or corrected evidence.
  A second rejection is final.
- Applications are decided within **5 business days** of submission.

## 3. Reason categories

| Category | Examples | Required documents |
|---|---|---|
| **Medical emergency** | Hospitalization, serious illness/injury of you or an immediate family member | Medical certificate or hospital record naming the patient |
| **Bereavement** | Death of an immediate family member | Death certificate or equivalent proof |
| **Visa rejection** | Destination-country visa denied despite timely application | Official rejection letter |
| **Force majeure** | Natural disaster, war, government advisory, or epidemic at the destination making the trip impossible or unsafe | Official advisory/news/government notice |
| **Change of plans** | Any other personal reason — no documents needed | None |

Documented emergencies (medical, bereavement, visa rejection, force majeure) are eligible
for **enhanced refunds at management discretion**, up to 100%, regardless of the timing
tiers below — when valid documents are provided.

## 4. Refund tiers (change of plans)

For standard applications, the refund percentage depends on **how many days before your
travel date the refund is approved**:

| Days before travel date (at application) | Refund |
|---|---|
| **30 days or more** | **90%** |
| **15 – 29 days** | **50%** |
| **7 – 14 days** | **25%** |
| **Fewer than 7 days** | **0%** |

Notes

- Your percentage is **locked in when you submit** the application — a slower review never
  moves you into a lower bracket. Applying early still maximizes your bracket.
- The refund amount is calculated on the amount actually paid for the booking.
- Any costs already paid by TripVerse to third parties on your behalf (visas, permits,
  insurance, non-recoverable supplier charges) may be deducted from the refundable amount.

## 5. When you get 100% back

A **full refund** is always issued — regardless of timing — when:

- **TripVerse or the tour operator cancels the trip** (including minimum group size not
  being met). You may alternatively accept an equivalent rescheduled trip if offered.
- **Force majeure at the destination** prevents the trip from taking place.
- Your refund application under a **documented emergency** category is verified and
  approved at the full rate.

## 6. No-shows and early departure

- Not showing up on the travel date without cancelling is treated as a **no-show**: no
  automatic refund. You may still apply under a documented-emergency category with proof.
- Leaving a trip after it has started does not entitle you to a refund for unused services.

## 7. How refunds are paid

- Approved amounts are returned to the **original payment method** used at booking
  (via our payment gateway, SSLCommerz).
- Processing takes up to **14 business days** after approval, depending on your bank/card
  issuer. Gateway processing fees, where applicable, are borne by the traveler.
- Refunds are never sent to a different card, account, or person than the one that paid.

## 8. Protect yourself further

Late cancellations fall hardest on travelers. We strongly recommend **travel insurance**
covering trip cancellation for illness and other unforeseen events — it is the intended
safety net for situations this policy cannot fully cover.

it speeds everything up.
